I am an academic in the Applied Optics Group, in Physics & Astronomy at the University of Kent. We are in Canterbury in the South East corner of England, about an hour on the train from London. It's quite nice, and I recommend a visit.

As well as research I also teach courses on the various physics and astronomy undergraduate degree programmes at Kent.

This site has links to papers and open source software, and copies of lecture notes from my physics teaching, plus the odd side project.
